Ask us
Frequently asked questions

In short.

Do I need a domain or hosting already?

No. If you do not have them, we help with the setup. The domain is registered for your business, and you receive access when the project is finished.

Do I pay you a monthly subscription?

No. There is no mandatory monthly fee or maintenance agreement with us. Domain and hosting costs are paid to the provider.

Can I have the website in English?

Yes. One language version — Norwegian or English — is included. If you want both, the second language version costs NOK 1,000.

Can you change the website later?

One simple business-data update is included within 12 months. Other changes can be agreed separately.

Will the site rank first on Google?

We do not promise search rankings. We build the site on a solid technical foundation so Google can find and understand it.

What is not included at these prices?

Online shops, custom booking systems, logins, advanced integrations, major redesigns and ongoing SEO or marketing are quoted separately.
